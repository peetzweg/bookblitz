bookblitz

Speed-read your own EPUBs, one word at a time.

Bring your own books. bookblitz reads EPUB files in your browser and keeps them, and your reading progress, on your device. There's no account, nothing is uploaded, and once it's on your Home Screen it works offline.

  • EPUB first: import from Files, iCloud Drive or your computer
  • Local first: your library lives in your browser's storage
  • Reading styles timed from speed-reading research, with a fixed focus letter
  • Drag up to read faster, down to slow down or read backwards
